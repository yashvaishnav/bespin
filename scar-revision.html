Scar Revision

Refining What Healing Left Behind

Advanced Scar Treatment for the Eyes & Face

Scars are an inevitable part of healing — but a scar that is raised, discolored, or poorly positioned doesn't have to be permanent. Whether from a previous surgery, an injury, or a skin condition, facial scars can affect both appearance and confidence in ways that are deeply personal.

Dr. Vaishnav combines precision laser therapy with targeted injectable treatments to soften, flatten, and improve the quality of scars around the eyes and face. His approach is grounded in the same anatomic expertise that guides his surgical work — treating the scar not as an isolated mark, but as part of the broader facial landscape.

Many patients see meaningful improvement within just a few treatment sessions, and most therapies require little to no downtime.

Treatment Options

How We Improve Scars

Every scar is different. Dr. Vaishnav tailors each treatment plan to the scar's type, location, and maturity — often combining approaches for the best possible result.

Laser Scar Resurfacing

Fractionated laser energy creates controlled micro-channels in the scar tissue, stimulating the body's own remodeling process. Over a series of treatments, scars become smoother, flatter, and closer in tone to the surrounding skin. When beneficial, topical therapies are applied during the laser session to further enhance the result.

Learn More

Injectable Scar Therapy

For raised or thickened scars, precisely placed injections can soften and flatten the tissue over time. These targeted treatments work by reducing excess collagen production and calming the inflammatory process that keeps certain scars elevated or firm. Sessions are brief, well-tolerated, and spaced to allow the scar to respond progressively.

Combined Approach

The most effective scar revision often involves a combination of laser and injectable therapies, timed strategically across the scar's healing timeline. Dr. Vaishnav creates a customized treatment plan that addresses texture, color, and contour — adapting as the scar evolves with each session.

What to Expect

Your Scar Revision Experience

Treatment begins with a thorough evaluation of the scar — its age, depth, texture, and how it interacts with the surrounding tissue. Dr. Vaishnav will discuss the realistic range of improvement and outline a treatment plan tailored to your specific scar.

Laser scar resurfacing uses fractionated energy to break down dense scar tissue in a controlled, precise manner. The laser creates thousands of microscopic treatment zones while leaving surrounding skin intact, which accelerates healing and minimizes downtime. In select cases, topical agents are applied during the session to penetrate the freshly treated channels and further improve the scar from within.

Injectable therapy involves small, carefully measured doses placed directly into the scar. These treatments work gradually — reducing the scar's thickness, firmness, and redness over a series of sessions spaced several weeks apart. The injections are quick, typically taking only a few minutes, and most patients find them very tolerable.

For many patients, the best results come from combining both approaches. Dr. Vaishnav will adjust the treatment plan at each visit based on how the scar is responding, ensuring steady progress without over-treating.

Most scar revision treatments are performed in the office with little to no downtime. After laser sessions, patients may experience mild redness and warmth for a day or two. Injectable treatments typically require no recovery time at all.

Quick Facts

  • Treatment Time: 15 to 45 minutes per session, depending on the scar and approach
  • Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream for laser; none or local anesthetic for injections
  • Sessions: Typically 3 to 6 sessions, spaced 4 to 6 weeks apart
  • Downtime: Minimal — mild redness for 1 to 2 days after laser; none after injections
  • Results: Progressive improvement with each session; optimal results at 3 to 6 months after completing treatment
  • Best Candidates: Patients with raised, thickened, red, or texturally irregular scars from surgery, trauma, or skin conditions

Timing depends on the type of treatment. Injectable therapies can often begin as early as four to six weeks after surgery, when the scar is still actively maturing — and in fact, early intervention can be especially effective during this window. Laser resurfacing is typically initiated once the incision is fully closed and any sutures have been removed, usually around six to eight weeks. Dr. Vaishnav will evaluate your scar's healing progress and recommend the optimal time to begin.

Both new and old scars can be treated. While early scars are often more responsive because the tissue is still remodeling, mature scars — even those that are years old — can see meaningful improvement with laser resurfacing and injectable therapy. The treatment approach may differ depending on the scar's age, but improvement is achievable in most cases.

Complete elimination of a scar is rarely possible — a scar represents the body's natural repair of disrupted tissue. However, scar revision can dramatically improve its appearance: reducing thickness, smoothing texture, fading discoloration, and softening the scar so it blends more naturally with the surrounding skin. The goal is a scar that is no longer noticeable at conversational distance.

Most patients find scar revision treatments very tolerable. For laser sessions, a topical numbing cream is applied beforehand, and most patients describe the sensation as a warm prickling. Injectable treatments involve a brief pinch and are typically over in a few minutes. Discomfort is minimal and short-lived with both approaches.
