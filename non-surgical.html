Treatment Options

Targeted Therapies, Refined Results

Each non-surgical treatment is selected and administered with the same anatomic rigor that defines Dr. Vaishnav's surgical practice — ensuring outcomes that are natural, proportionate, and aligned with your individual facial structure.

Neuromodulators

Botulinum toxin injections — including Botox and Dysport — selectively relax the facial muscles responsible for dynamic wrinkles such as crow's feet, frown lines, and horizontal forehead creases. Dr. Vaishnav's understanding of periorbital and facial musculature allows for precise dosing and placement that softens expression lines while preserving natural movement and avoiding a frozen appearance.

Dermal Fillers

Hyaluronic acid-based dermal fillers restore volume lost through aging, smooth static lines, and enhance facial contour. Common treatment areas include the tear troughs, temples, cheeks, nasolabial folds, and lips. As an oculofacial surgeon, Dr. Vaishnav has an exceptional understanding of the deep and superficial fat compartments and vascular anatomy that govern safe, precise filler placement.

Laser Skin Resurfacing

What to Expect

Your Non-Surgical Treatment Experience

Neuromodulators (Botox & Dysport)

Neuromodulator treatments begin with a thorough evaluation of your facial dynamics at rest and during expression. Dr. Vaishnav identifies the specific muscles contributing to unwanted lines and develops an individualized injection plan that targets those muscles with precision. The procedure itself takes approximately 10 to 15 minutes and involves a series of small injections using an ultrafine needle. Most patients report only minimal discomfort. Results become apparent within 3 to 7 days, with full effect at two weeks, and typically last 3 to 4 months.

Dermal Fillers

Filler treatments begin with an assessment of facial volume, symmetry, and proportions. Dr. Vaishnav selects the appropriate filler product based on the treatment area and desired outcome — thinner, more fluid formulations for delicate areas such as the tear troughs and lips, and more structured gels for volumizing the cheeks, temples, and jawline. Topical anesthetic or nerve blocks are applied for comfort. The procedure typically takes 20 to 45 minutes, with results visible immediately. Most hyaluronic acid fillers last 6 to 18 months depending on the product and treatment area.

Laser Skin Resurfacing

Laser treatments are preceded by a skin evaluation to determine the most appropriate wavelength, depth, and intensity for your concerns and skin type. Depending on the laser modality selected, a topical numbing cream or local anesthetic is applied prior to treatment. Sessions typically range from 15 to 45 minutes. Non-ablative treatments involve minimal downtime with mild redness lasting one to three days, while ablative resurfacing may require five to ten days of recovery as the skin heals and regenerates. A series of treatments may be recommended for optimal results, and Dr. Vaishnav provides detailed post-procedure skincare instructions to support healing and maximize benefit.

Quick Facts

  • Neuromodulators: 10 to 15 minutes; no downtime; results in 3 to 7 days, lasting 3 to 4 months
  • Dermal Fillers: 20 to 45 minutes; minimal downtime; immediate results lasting 6 to 18 months
  • Laser Resurfacing: 15 to 45 minutes; downtime varies from 1 to 10 days depending on modality
  • Anesthesia: Topical numbing cream, ice, or local anesthetic as appropriate
  • Activity Restrictions: Avoid strenuous exercise for 24 to 48 hours after injectables; follow specific post-laser protocols as directed
  • Maintenance: Neuromodulators every 3 to 4 months; fillers every 6 to 18 months; laser treatments may be periodic based on goals

Frequently Asked Questions

Oculofacial plastic surgeons complete years of specialized fellowship training focused exclusively on the anatomy and aesthetics of the eyelids, orbits, and surrounding facial structures. This level of anatomic expertise is directly relevant to injectable and laser treatments, where precise knowledge of muscle layers, vascular anatomy, and fat compartments determines both safety and quality of outcome. Dr. Vaishnav's surgical background means he understands the deeper structures beneath the skin — an advantage that translates to more natural results, lower complication rates, and the ability to manage any rare adverse event with confidence and surgical skill.

Neuromodulators and dermal fillers address fundamentally different concerns and are often complementary. Neuromodulators such as Botox and Dysport are most effective for dynamic wrinkles — lines that appear with facial expression, including crow's feet, frown lines, and forehead creases. Dermal fillers restore lost volume, smooth static lines that are visible at rest, and enhance facial contour. During your consultation, Dr. Vaishnav will analyze your facial anatomy and aging patterns to recommend the most appropriate treatment or combination of treatments tailored to your specific goals.

When performed by a qualified physician with thorough knowledge of facial anatomy, non-surgical treatments have an excellent safety profile. Common side effects of injectables include mild bruising, swelling, and tenderness at the injection site, which typically resolve within a few days. Rare but more serious complications — such as vascular occlusion from filler or unintended muscle diffusion from neuromodulators — underscore the importance of selecting an experienced, anatomically trained injector. Laser treatments may cause temporary redness, swelling, and skin sensitivity. Dr. Vaishnav discusses all potential risks and expected recovery in detail prior to any treatment.

For example, blepharoplasty may be paired with neuromodulators to soften crow's feet, or facial surgery may be enhanced with filler to restore subtle volume in areas such as the temples or tear troughs. Laser resurfacing can improve skin quality and texture following surgical rejuvenation. Dr. Vaishnav's dual expertise in both surgical and non-surgical modalities allows him to develop a comprehensive, integrated treatment plan that achieves the most harmonious and complete result.

The timeline for results varies by treatment. Neuromodulators begin to take effect within 3 to 7 days, with full results at approximately two weeks, and typically last 3 to 4 months. Dermal fillers produce immediate visible improvement that continues to refine over one to two weeks as any swelling subsides; results last 6 to 18 months depending on the product used and the treatment area. Laser resurfacing results develop gradually as collagen remodeling occurs over several weeks to months following treatment, with improvements in skin quality that can be long-lasting when supported by proper sun protection and skincare.
