Surgery Designed for the Most Delicate Structures of the Face

The eyelids are among the thinnest, most complex tissues in the human body. They protect the eye, facilitate vision, and play a defining role in facial expression. When aging, genetics, or medical conditions alter their form or function, the consequences extend beyond appearance — they can affect how clearly you see and how rested you feel.

His fellowship training in ophthalmic plastic and reconstructive surgery provides a depth of anatomic understanding that is essential for achieving results that are both functionally sound and aesthetically refined.

Whether your concern is cosmetic, functional, or both, Dr. Vaishnav approaches every procedure with meticulous attention to the interplay between eyelid architecture, orbital anatomy, and the natural harmony of your face.

Eyelid Procedures

Tailored to Your Anatomy, Your Goals

Each eyelid procedure is carefully planned to address your specific concerns — whether you seek a more refreshed appearance, improved peripheral vision, or correction of a drooping eyelid.

Upper Blepharoplasty

Upper eyelid surgery removes excess skin and, when indicated, sculpts underlying fat to restore a natural, youthful eyelid contour. Often performed for both cosmetic rejuvenation and functional improvement when redundant skin impairs the superior visual field.

Learn More

Lower Blepharoplasty

Lower eyelid surgery addresses under-eye bags, hollowing, and skin laxity by repositioning or removing herniated orbital fat and tightening supportive structures. The result is a smoother, more rested lower eyelid-cheek junction without an operated appearance.

Learn More

Eyelid Ptosis Repair

Ptosis repair corrects a drooping upper eyelid caused by weakening or dehiscence of the levator muscle. By restoring proper eyelid height and symmetry, ptosis surgery improves both the visual axis and the balanced, alert appearance of the eyes.

What to Expect

Your Eyelid Surgery Experience

Every eyelid procedure at San Diego Eye Plastic Surgeons begins with a thorough consultation. Dr. Vaishnav performs a comprehensive ophthalmic and facial assessment, including eyelid measurements, visual field testing when indicated, and a detailed discussion of your concerns and goals. This evaluation ensures that the surgical plan is precisely tailored to your anatomy.

Upper blepharoplasty is performed through an incision carefully placed within the natural eyelid crease, rendering it virtually imperceptible once healed. Excess skin, orbicularis muscle, and protruding fat are addressed as needed to create a clean, defined upper lid with preserved natural fullness.

Lower blepharoplasty may be approached through a transconjunctival incision — made on the inside of the eyelid with no visible external scar — or through a subciliary incision just beneath the lash line. Dr. Vaishnav selects the approach best suited to each patient's anatomy, often incorporating fat repositioning to smooth the transition between the lower eyelid and cheek.

Ptosis repair involves reinforcing or reattaching the levator aponeurosis, the tendon-like tissue responsible for elevating the upper eyelid. In cases of severe ptosis, a frontalis suspension technique may be employed. The goal is symmetric, natural-appearing eyelid height that restores both function and aesthetics.

All eyelid procedures are typically performed on an outpatient basis, and many patients are able to return to most daily activities within one to two weeks. Dr. Vaishnav and his team provide detailed preoperative and postoperative guidance to support a smooth, comfortable recovery.

Quick Facts

  • Procedure Time: 45 minutes to 2 hours, depending on the scope of surgery
  • Anesthesia: Local anesthesia with sedation; general anesthesia available if preferred
  • Recovery: 7 to 14 days for initial healing; sutures removed at approximately one week
  • Activity Restrictions: Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for 2 to 3 weeks
  • Results: Visible improvement within 2 to 4 weeks; final results at 3 to 6 months as tissues settle
  • Longevity: Results are long-lasting, typically 7 to 15 years for blepharoplasty; ptosis repair outcomes are generally permanent
Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Blepharoplasty addresses excess skin, fat, and tissue of the upper or lower eyelids — it reshapes the eyelid to create a more youthful, rested contour. Ptosis repair is a distinct procedure that corrects a drooping eyelid caused by weakening of the levator muscle, the muscle responsible for opening the eye. While both procedures involve the upper eyelid, they address different anatomic problems and require different surgical techniques. In some patients, blepharoplasty and ptosis repair are performed together for optimal results.

Upper blepharoplasty and ptosis repair may be covered by medical insurance when the condition is documented as functionally significant — meaning the excess skin or drooping eyelid measurably impairs your visual field. Dr. Vaishnav's office will perform the necessary clinical testing and work with your insurance provider to determine eligibility before surgery. Lower blepharoplasty is typically considered a cosmetic procedure and is not covered by insurance. During your consultation, we will provide a clear understanding of anticipated costs and available options.

In the first few days following surgery, patients can expect mild swelling and bruising around the eyelids, which is managed with cold compresses, head elevation, and prescribed ointment. Most patients describe the discomfort as minimal. Sutures are typically removed at approximately one week. The majority of visible bruising and swelling resolves within 10 to 14 days, and many patients feel comfortable returning to work and social activities at that time. Strenuous exercise should be avoided for two to three weeks. Final results continue to refine over the following months as the tissues heal and settle into their new contour.

One of the advantages of eyelid surgery is that incisions are placed in naturally concealed locations. For upper blepharoplasty, the incision follows the natural eyelid crease and becomes virtually invisible once fully healed. For lower blepharoplasty, a transconjunctival approach leaves no external scar at all, as the incision is made inside the eyelid. When an external lower lid incision is necessary, it is placed just beneath the lash line and typically fades to an imperceptible line within a few months. Dr. Vaishnav's precise surgical technique and careful closure further minimize any scarring.

Ideal candidates for eyelid surgery are individuals in good general health who are bothered by excess upper eyelid skin, puffiness or bags beneath the eyes, or drooping of the upper eyelid. Candidates may seek improvement for cosmetic reasons, functional concerns such as impaired peripheral vision, or both. During your consultation, Dr. Vaishnav will evaluate your eyelid anatomy, discuss your goals, review your medical and ocular history, and help determine which procedure — or combination of procedures — will best address your concerns. Certain conditions such as dry eye, thyroid eye disease, or prior eyelid surgery require careful consideration and will be discussed in detail.
