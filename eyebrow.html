Brow & Forehead

Restoring the Upper Face

Brow Position Is Central to Facial Expression and Periorbital Harmony

The brow and forehead form the upper framework of the face. Even subtle changes in brow position — a descent of a few millimeters, deepening of horizontal forehead lines, or heaviness in the lateral brow — can produce an appearance of fatigue, displeasure, or premature aging that does not reflect how you feel. In many cases, what patients perceive as excess upper eyelid skin is, in part, the result of brow ptosis weighing on the lid below.

Dr. Yash Vaishnav is a board-certified oculofacial plastic surgeon with specialized training in the anatomy of the brow, forehead, and periorbital region. His deep understanding of the interplay between brow position, eyelid mechanics, and forehead musculature allows him to develop surgical plans that address the true source of each patient's concern — not merely its visible effect.

Whether performed in isolation or in conjunction with upper blepharoplasty for a comprehensive periorbital rejuvenation, brow and forehead surgery under Dr. Vaishnav's care is designed to produce results that are balanced, natural, and enduring.

Brow & Forehead Procedures

Precision Techniques for Lasting Elevation

Dr. Vaishnav selects the brow lift technique best suited to each patient's anatomy, degree of ptosis, hairline position, and aesthetic goals. Every approach is executed with meticulous attention to symmetry, natural brow contour, and the preservation of expressive movement.

Endoscopic Brow Lift

The endoscopic brow lift is a minimally invasive approach performed through several small incisions concealed behind the hairline. Using a thin endoscope and specialized instruments, Dr. Vaishnav releases the tissues responsible for brow descent, repositions the brow to a more youthful height, and secures it with absorbable fixation. This technique offers reduced scarring, shorter recovery, and natural-appearing elevation.

Learn More

Direct & Temporal Brow Lift

For patients who require targeted correction of the lateral brow or who may not be ideal candidates for an endoscopic approach, Dr. Vaishnav offers direct and temporal brow lift techniques. A direct brow lift places the incision at or just above the brow, while a temporal lift addresses the outer brow through incisions within the temporal hairline. Both approaches provide precise, predictable elevation where it is needed most.

Forehead Rejuvenation

Forehead rejuvenation addresses deep horizontal lines, glabellar furrows between the brows, and generalized forehead heaviness. By modifying the activity of the frontalis and corrugator muscles during brow lift surgery, Dr. Vaishnav smooths persistent creases while preserving the patient's natural range of expression. This may also be combined with non-surgical treatments for optimal, long-lasting results.

What to Expect

Your Brow Lift Experience

Every brow and forehead procedure at San Diego Eye Plastic Surgeons begins with a detailed consultation. Dr. Vaishnav performs a comprehensive assessment of brow position, forehead anatomy, hairline characteristics, and the relationship between the brow and upper eyelid. Standardized measurements and photographs document the degree of brow ptosis and asymmetry, ensuring the surgical plan is precisely calibrated to your facial structure.

For endoscopic brow lifts, three to five small incisions are placed behind the hairline. Through these access points, Dr. Vaishnav uses an endoscope to visualize the deep tissues of the forehead, release the periosteum and corrugator muscles contributing to brow descent and frown lines, and elevate the brow complex to its optimal position. The brow is then secured with bioabsorbable fixation devices that dissolve over several months as the tissue heals in its new location.

Direct and temporal brow lifts are performed through carefully planned incisions — at the brow margin, within a forehead crease, or along the temporal hairline — depending on the patient's anatomy and the area requiring correction. These approaches allow for targeted, highly controlled elevation and are particularly well suited to patients with significant lateral brow ptosis or those with a high hairline.

When brow ptosis contributes to upper eyelid heaviness, Dr. Vaishnav frequently combines a brow lift with upper blepharoplasty to address both the brow and eyelid in a single session. This combined approach treats the underlying cause and its visible effect, producing a more complete and harmonious rejuvenation than either procedure alone.

Brow lift surgery is performed on an outpatient basis. Most patients are able to resume light daily activities within seven to ten days, with full recovery over the following weeks. Dr. Vaishnav provides thorough preoperative and postoperative instructions to ensure a comfortable healing process and optimal outcome.

Quick Facts

  • Procedure Time: 1 to 2 hours, depending on the technique and whether combined with blepharoplasty
  • Anesthesia: Local anesthesia with intravenous sedation; general anesthesia available for combined procedures
  • Recovery: 7 to 14 days for initial healing; most sutures or fixation devices are absorbable
  • Activity Restrictions: Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for 3 to 4 weeks
  • Results: Noticeable improvement within 2 to 3 weeks; final results at 3 to 6 months as swelling fully resolves
  • Longevity: Results are long-lasting, typically 8 to 12 years, with continued benefit as aging progresses more gracefully from the elevated position

Frequently Asked Questions

Many patients who believe they need upper blepharoplasty actually have brow ptosis — a descent of the brow that pushes excess tissue onto the eyelid. In other cases, both conditions are present simultaneously. During your consultation, Dr. Vaishnav carefully evaluates brow position relative to the orbital rim and assesses the relationship between the brow and upper eyelid. By manually elevating the brow to its ideal position, he can determine how much of the upper lid fullness is attributable to the brow versus the eyelid itself. This analysis ensures the correct procedure — or combination of procedures — is recommended.

A natural result is the foremost priority. An over-elevated or uniformly raised brow produces the startled, unnatural appearance that many patients rightly wish to avoid. Dr. Vaishnav plans each brow lift to restore the brow to an anatomically appropriate position — not an artificially high one. He pays particular attention to the differential elevation of the medial, central, and lateral brow segments, preserving the natural arch and subtle asymmetry that characterize a genuine, expressive face. The goal is to look refreshed and rested, not altered.

Scarring depends on the technique used. With an endoscopic brow lift, the small incisions are hidden entirely behind the hairline and are virtually undetectable once healed. A temporal brow lift also places incisions within the hair-bearing scalp, concealing them effectively. A direct brow lift, while placing the incision near the brow itself, is meticulously closed and typically heals as a fine line that can be camouflaged within a natural forehead crease or along the superior brow border. Dr. Vaishnav discusses the specific incision placement and expected scarring for each technique during your consultation so you can make a fully informed decision.

Yes. A brow lift is frequently performed in conjunction with upper blepharoplasty to achieve comprehensive periorbital rejuvenation. Addressing both the brow and the eyelid in a single operative session treats the full spectrum of upper facial aging and often produces a more harmonious result than either procedure alone. A brow lift may also be combined with lower blepharoplasty, midface procedures, or non-surgical treatments such as neuromodulators and dermal fillers. Dr. Vaishnav will recommend the combination best suited to your anatomy and goals during your consultation.

Most patients experience mild to moderate swelling and bruising in the forehead and upper eyelid region, which typically peaks within the first two to three days and subsides progressively over one to two weeks. Some temporary numbness or tightness in the forehead is common and resolves as the sensory nerves recover. Discomfort is generally well controlled with prescribed medications. A light dressing or wrap may be placed for the first day or two. Most patients return to work and light activities within 7 to 10 days. Strenuous exercise and heavy lifting should be avoided for three to four weeks. Dr. Vaishnav provides detailed recovery instructions and follows your progress closely through scheduled postoperative visits.
