Meet Your Surgeon

Dr. Yash Vaishnav

Oculofacial Plastic Surgeon

A Surgeon Devoted to the Eyes & Face

Dr. Yash Vaishnav is a board-certified oculofacial plastic surgeon whose practice is dedicated exclusively to the aesthetic and reconstructive surgery of the eyelids, brows, and face.

His advanced fellowship training in ophthalmic plastic and reconstructive surgery provides an unparalleled understanding of the intricate anatomy surrounding the eyes — enabling results that are both beautiful and anatomically sound.

Dr. Vaishnav's approach is rooted in careful listening, meticulous surgical planning, and an unwavering commitment to outcomes that honor each patient's individual features.

Philosophy

The Approach

Great results are never one-size-fits-all. Dr. Vaishnav believes that the most elegant outcomes emerge from a deep understanding of each patient's anatomy, goals, and the subtle details that make their face uniquely theirs.

Precision

Fellowship-trained expertise in the delicate periorbital anatomy, where millimeters make the difference between good and exceptional.

Artistry

An aesthetic sensibility refined through years of dedicated study — understanding not just how to operate, but how beauty and balance relate to each face.

Trust

Honest, thorough consultations where you are heard. Dr. Vaishnav will only recommend a procedure if he believes it will genuinely benefit you.

Training & Credentials

Education & Fellowship

Dr. Vaishnav's path to oculofacial plastic surgery reflects years of rigorous, specialized training at leading institutions:

  • Fellowship in Ophthalmic Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery
  • Residency in Ophthalmology
  • Doctor of Medicine
  • Board Certified — American Board of Ophthalmology
