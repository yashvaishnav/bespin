Welcome

A Practice Dedicated to the Eyes & Face

San Diego Eye Plastic Surgeons is the practice of Dr. Yash Vaishnav, a board-certified oculofacial plastic surgeon who focuses exclusively on surgical and non-surgical procedures of the eyelids, brows, and face.

With specialized fellowship training in ophthalmic plastic and reconstructive surgery, Dr. Vaishnav brings a depth of anatomic knowledge and surgical precision that is uniquely suited to the delicate periorbital region.
