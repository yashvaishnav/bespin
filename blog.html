From Dr. Vaishnav

Knowledge as a Foundation for Confident Decisions

Dr. Yash Vaishnav believes that informed patients are empowered patients. Understanding your anatomy, your options, and the reasoning behind each recommendation is essential to achieving results that truly align with your goals. This blog is an extension of that philosophy — a curated collection of educational articles designed to demystify oculofacial plastic surgery, clarify the distinctions between procedures, and offer thoughtful guidance as you consider your path forward. Whether you are beginning your research or preparing for a scheduled consultation, we hope these insights provide clarity and confidence.

Understanding Upper Eyelid Surgery: What to Expect

Upper blepharoplasty is one of the most commonly performed oculofacial procedures — yet many patients are unsure what it entails. From the initial consultation through recovery, this article walks you through each stage of the process, the subtle decisions your surgeon makes, and what realistic outcomes look like.

December 8, 2025

Brow Ptosis vs. Eyelid Ptosis: Why the Distinction Matters

A heavy or drooping appearance around the eyes is not always caused by the eyelid itself. Brow descent can mimic eyelid ptosis, and treating the wrong structure leads to suboptimal results. Learn how an oculofacial specialist differentiates between these conditions and why accurate diagnosis is the cornerstone of effective treatment.

November 20, 2025

The Role of an Oculofacial Plastic Surgeon

Oculofacial plastic surgery sits at the intersection of ophthalmology and plastic surgery — a highly specialized discipline focused exclusively on the eyelids, orbits, tear ducts, and surrounding facial structures. Discover what distinguishes this training pathway and why it matters when choosing a surgeon for periorbital procedures.

October 12, 2025

What Is Ptosis and How Is It Treated?

Ptosis — a drooping of the upper eyelid — can develop at any age and may affect one or both eyes. Whether caused by age-related changes, prior surgery, or a congenital condition, ptosis can impair vision and alter facial symmetry. This article explores the types, causes, and modern surgical approaches used to restore natural eyelid position.

September 5, 2025

Choosing the Right Time for Eyelid Surgery

Patients often ask whether there is an ideal age or season for eyelid surgery. The answer is nuanced, depending on the nature of the concern, lifestyle factors, and the patient's overall health. Dr. Vaishnav shares his perspective on how to determine when the time is right — and when patience may be the better course.

August 18, 2025

Non-Surgical Options for Periorbital Rejuvenation

Not every concern around the eyes requires surgery. From neuromodulators and dermal fillers to laser resurfacing and medical-grade skincare, a range of non-surgical treatments can address fine lines, volume loss, and skin texture changes. Learn which options may be appropriate for your goals and how they complement surgical care.
